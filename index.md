I work on getting good structures out of neutron and X-ray diffraction data. At the [European Spallation Source](https://www.ess.eu/) I develop the data reduction and analysis side of **NMX**, a time-of-flight Laue instrument for neutron macromolecular crystallography. Before ESS I spent a decade as a beamline scientist and postdoc at synchrotrons (MAX IV, CHESS, the Swiss Light Source), working on serial and room-temperature crystallography and on experimental phasing.
